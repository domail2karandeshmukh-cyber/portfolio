# Publish Your First Document

This draft breaks the style guide on purpose, so you can watch the check work. It takes about two minutes.

## Before you start:

- You do not need an account, and nothing you type leaves your browser.
- Please keep the text on the left in view - it is the source of everything on the right.

## Steps

1. Click on "Style check" to see what {{tool}} found
2. Simply select **Fix** next to a finding, e.g. the first one.
3. Look at the preview, which will show the corrected text.
4. Select **Publish**, and then choose Markdown, HTML or DITA.

::: only writer

## For writers

A line that starts with `::: only writer` opens a block for one group of readers. Choose a reader above the preview to see the document with and without the block.

:::

## Check that it worked

The seal above the preview says **Passes the style check**!
