How to reset 2FA

Sometimes users lose their phone and can't login anymore. This is a piece of cake to fix!

Steps:

1. Login to Keycloak and go to users.

2. Find the user and click on credentials tab.

3. Simply delete the OTP and the user will be asked to setup 2FA again on their next login.

You can change the MFA settings here if needed.

Note: codes are valid 30 secs.

If it still doesn't work please contact IT.
