# A starter's laptop hasn't arrived

The laptop is the one part of onboarding that travels. When it hasn't arrived on the first morning, the person can still start the day, and you can find out where it's got to in a few minutes.

## Find the shipment

1. Open the onboarding request in the Service Desk portal. The **Hardware** section shows the order number and the carrier's tracking link.
2. If the tracking shows a delivery at another site, ask that site's reception to hold it, and ask hardware logistics in the **Hardware** queue to arrange a courier. Don't ask the person to collect it themselves.
3. If there's no order number, the order wasn't placed. Raise it in the **Hardware** queue with the request number and the tag **Starter**, which puts it first in line.

## Let the person start anyway

- A loan laptop from the site's pool works with their account the moment it's enrolled. Ask reception for one and enrol it the same way: the person signs in and the device registers itself.
- Without any device, the person can enrol their account and read the intranet from a phone. The mailbox and the chat tool work there too.
- When the right laptop arrives, the person signs in to it, and the loan laptop goes back to the pool. Nothing needs to be moved between the two.

## Who to tell

Tell the person's manager what happened and when the laptop is expected. Tell hardware logistics if the same site has lost a second shipment this quarter, so that the delivery address for the site can be checked.
