# Request access to an application

Any application outside your department's standard set needs a request, so that the application's owner can choose the right role and the record shows who approved it.

## Steps

1. In the Service Desk portal, select **Request access**.
2. Choose the application from the catalogue. If it isn't listed, select **An application that isn't listed** and name it: the desk finds the owner.
3. Choose the person. You can request for yourself or for someone who reports to you.
4. Write the reason in one or two sentences, in terms of the work: "approves supplier invoices for the Rotterdam site", not "needs access".
5. If the access is temporary, set an end date. Temporary access is removed on its own and needs no follow-up.
6. Select **Submit**.

## What happens next

Your manager approves first, unless you're requesting for your own report. Then the application owner approves and chooses the role. The desk grants the access within a working day of the last approval and the portal tells you. The whole path usually takes two to three working days; a privileged role takes up to a week because the security team reviews it.

## If it's declined

The portal shows who declined and their comment. Most declines ask for a narrower role or a clearer reason. Change the request and submit it again rather than opening a new one, so that the history stays in one place.
