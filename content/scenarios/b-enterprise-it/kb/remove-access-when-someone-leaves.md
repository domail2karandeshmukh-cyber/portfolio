# Remove access when someone leaves

Access ends on the last working day, not when HR gets round to it and not when the manager remembers. The leaver's request, raised by HR, drives everything below.

## Before the last day

1. HR raises a **Leaver** request with the last working day. The desk schedules the account to deactivate at 18:00 local time on that day.
2. The manager says in the request who takes over the mailbox and the files. A mailbox is forwarded for thirty days to one person; files in the home drive move to the manager.
3. The desk lists the leaver's requested access in the request, so that the manager can see which applications need a replacement owner.

## On the last day

1. The account deactivates at 18:00. Every session ends, and the laptop can't be unlocked with the account after a restart.
2. The leaver returns the laptop and badge to reception, who record it in the request.
3. Privileged roles end at the same moment. For a role that runs a shared service, the application owner confirms in the request that another person now holds it.

## After the last day

- Thirty days later, the mailbox forwarding ends and the mailbox is archived.
- Ninety days later, the account is deleted. Until then, HR can ask for it to be reactivated if the person returns.

## When someone leaves at once

For a dismissal or a security case, HR or the security team raises the leaver request with **Immediate** set. The desk deactivates the account within fifteen minutes, before the person is told. The laptop and badge are collected in the meeting.
