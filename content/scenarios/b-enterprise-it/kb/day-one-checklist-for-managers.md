# Day-one checklist for managers

What to have ready before a new team member's first day, and what to do during it. The Service Desk handles the accounts; this list is the part only you can do.

## The week before

- Confirm in the onboarding request that the laptop's delivery address is right. A laptop sent to the wrong site costs three days.
- Check that every application you asked for has been approved by its owner. The request shows each one as **Approved**, **Declined**, or **Waiting**. Chase a **Waiting** owner yourself: they answer their peers faster than the desk.
- Book the first week: a welcome meeting, a session with each person they'll work with, and time alone to set up.
- Name a buddy for the first month, and tell them.

## The first morning

1. Meet the person at reception, or online at the agreed time.
2. Hand over the laptop, and sit with them while they open the welcome message and enrol. The enrolment asks them to set a password and a second factor; it takes ten minutes.
3. Check that they can open their mailbox, the intranet, and the chat tool. Those three come with the department group.
4. Open the applications you requested together. If one is missing, open a ticket from the onboarding request and not a new one, so that the desk sees the history.

## By the end of the week

- Confirm the first-day checklist in the Service Desk portal. The request can't close without it.
- Ask the person what they still can't reach. Request it through the portal, with the reason, so that the owner can decide.
