# Unlock a customer account

Internal procedure for support agents. A customer asks you to unlock their account because they can't wait for the lock to lift, or because an administrator isn't available. Verify who you're talking to first: see *Verify a customer's identity before changing sign-in settings*.

## Before you start

- The customer has passed identity verification in this conversation, and you have noted the method in the ticket.
- You have the **Support agent** role in the support console.

## Steps

1. In the support console, search for the account by email address.
2. On the **Sign-in** tab, check the lock: its start time, how many wrong attempts caused it, and the locations of the attempts.
3. If the attempts come from a location the customer doesn't recognise, don't unlock. Reset the password instead (step 5) and advise two-step verification.
4. If the customer recognises the attempts, select **Unlock**, and then enter the ticket number as the reason.
5. If the customer isn't sure of the password, select **Send a reset link** instead. The link unlocks the account when it's used.
6. Record in the ticket what you did and why.

## Check that it worked

The **Sign-in** tab shows **Unlocked by** with your name and the ticket number, and the customer can sign in. The customer receives an email that the account was unlocked by support.

## Never do this

- Never set a password for a customer, or read one out. Agents can't see passwords, and the console won't let you set one.
- Never unlock on the request of someone who isn't the account holder or an administrator of the company, however urgent the request sounds.
