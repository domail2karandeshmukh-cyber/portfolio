# Connect a bank feed

A bank feed brings your transactions into Ledgerly every night, so that reconciling is a matter of confirming matches instead of typing. You connect it once and confirm it again when your bank asks, usually every 90 days.

## Before you start

- You can sign in to your bank's online banking.
- You're an administrator or a bookkeeper in Ledgerly.

## Steps

1. Select **Banking**, and then select **Add a bank**.
2. Search for your bank and select it.
3. Sign in to your bank in the window that opens. The window belongs to your bank's connection service: Ledgerly never sees your banking password.
4. Choose the accounts to share, and then confirm.
5. Back in Ledgerly, match each bank account to a Ledgerly account, or select **Create a new account**.
6. Select **Start importing**.

## Check that it worked

Each account shows **Connected** and a count of imported transactions. The first import brings the last 90 days and takes a few minutes. From then on, new transactions appear each morning under **Banking** > **To review**.

## What Ledgerly can and can't do with the connection

The connection is read-only. Ledgerly can see balances and transactions, and nothing else. It can't make payments, change your bank details, or see your banking password. You can remove the connection at any time under **Banking** > **Disconnect**, and from your bank's own settings.
