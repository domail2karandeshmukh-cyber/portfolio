# Upcoming change: new sign-in page in January

From 12 January 2027, the Ledgerly sign-in page looks different. Nothing about your account changes: the address, your email, your password, and your two-step verification all stay the same.

## What looks different

- The page asks for your email address first, and then for your password or your company's sign-in. People who use single sign-on no longer see a password field they can't use.
- The **Forgot your password?** link is under the password field instead of the bottom of the page.
- The page is in the language of your browser. Before, it was always in English.

## What stays the same

- Your email address and password.
- Two-step verification and your backup codes.
- Sessions you're signed in to. You aren't signed out by the change.

## What to tell your team

If you manage a team, tell them the date and that the page looks different, so that nobody mistakes the new page for a fake one. The address stays `app.ledgerly.example`, and the page always shows the padlock in the browser's address bar.
