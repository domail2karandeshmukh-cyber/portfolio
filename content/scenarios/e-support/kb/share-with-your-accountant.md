# Share your books with your accountant

Give your accountant their own access instead of your password. They see what their role allows, their changes are recorded under their name, and you can remove the access at any time.

## Steps

1. Select **Settings** > **Users**, and then select **Invite**.
2. Enter your accountant's email address.
3. Under **Role**, choose **Accountant**. The role can see and edit every transaction and report, and can't change billing, users, or bank connections.
4. Select **Send invitation**.

## Check that it worked

Your accountant receives an email and appears under **Users** with the status **Invited** until they accept, then **Active**. Their changes show their name in the audit log under **Settings** > **Activity**.

## At the end of the engagement

Under **Settings** > **Users**, select the accountant, and then select **Remove**. Everything they entered stays in your books.
