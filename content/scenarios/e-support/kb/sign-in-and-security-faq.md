# Sign-in and security: questions and answers

## Do I need two-step verification?

No, but we recommend it. With it, a password alone isn't enough to get into your books. Turn it on under **Your account** > **Security**. Administrators can require it for everyone in the company.

## Which authenticator apps work?

Any app that generates time-based codes, such as the ones built into most password managers. Ledgerly shows a QR code to scan when you set it up.

## I lost my phone. How do I sign in?

Use one of the backup codes you saved when you set up two-step verification. If you have none, ask your administrator to reset your second factor. They'll check who you are first.

## Why was I signed out?

Ledgerly signs you out after 14 days, after a password change, and when an administrator removes a device. If that happens more often, check that your browser keeps cookies for ledgerly.example.

## Can I see where my account is signed in?

Yes. **Your account** > **Sign-in activity** lists every device, with its last activity and approximate location. Select **Sign out** next to any device you don't recognise, and then reset your password.

## Does the support team ever ask for my password by email?

Never. A message that asks for your password, a code, or a payment to keep your account isn't from us. Forward it to security at ledgerly.example and delete it.
