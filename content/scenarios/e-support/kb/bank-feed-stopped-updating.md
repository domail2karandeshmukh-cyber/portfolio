# A bank feed stopped updating

Transactions stop arriving when the bank asks you to confirm the connection again, which most banks do every 90 days, or when your bank sign-in details change.

## What you see

Under **Banking**, the account shows **Needs attention**, and the last transaction is more than two days old.

## Fix it

1. Under **Banking**, select the account, and then select **Reconnect**.
2. Sign in to your bank in the window that opens, and confirm that Ledgerly may read the account. Ledgerly can't move money.
3. Wait for the status to change to **Connected**. The missing transactions arrive within an hour.

## If the bank isn't listed any more

Some banks change the connection they offer. Select **Add a bank**, search for your bank again, and connect it as a new account. Then select **Merge with** on the old account so that your history stays in one place.

## If transactions are missing after reconnecting

Feeds import the last 90 days. For older transactions, download a statement file from your bank and import it under **Banking** > **Import a statement**. Ledgerly skips transactions it already has.
