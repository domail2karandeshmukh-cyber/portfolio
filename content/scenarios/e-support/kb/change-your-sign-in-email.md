# Change the email address you sign in with

Your sign-in email is also where Ledgerly sends receipts, reminders, and security messages, so changing it takes two confirmations: one from the old address and one from the new one.

## Steps

1. Select your name in the top-right corner, and then select **Your account**.
2. Select **Change** next to **Email**.
3. Enter the new address, and then select **Send confirmations**.
4. Open the message sent to your old address and select **Yes, change it**. If you no longer have access to the old address, ask your administrator to change the email for you instead.
5. Open the message sent to the new address and select **Confirm this address**.

## Check that it worked

**Your account** shows the new address, and you sign in with it from now on. Invoices you've already sent keep the address they were sent from.
