# Set up recurring invoices

For a customer you bill the same amount every month or every quarter, create the invoice once and let Ledgerly send it on schedule. You can review each one before it goes, or let it go on its own.

## Steps

1. Open an invoice for the customer, or create one, and then select **Make recurring**.
2. Under **Repeat**, choose how often and on which day.
3. Under **Starts** and **Ends**, set the first invoice date and, if there's one, the last. Leave **Ends** empty for an open-ended schedule.
4. Under **Sending**, choose **Send automatically** or **Create as a draft for me to review**.
5. Select **Save schedule**.

## Check that it worked

The invoice shows a **Recurring** badge, and **Sales** > **Recurring** lists the schedule with its next date. On that date, the new invoice appears under **Sales** > **Invoices**, sent or as a draft, depending on what you chose.

## Changing the amount

Edit the schedule under **Sales** > **Recurring**. The change applies to the next invoice and the ones after it. Invoices already sent don't change.
