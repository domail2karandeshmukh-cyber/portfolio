# Send payment reminders automatically

Ledgerly can remind a customer before and after an invoice is due, with wording you choose. Reminders stop on their own when the invoice is paid.

## Steps

1. Select **Settings** > **Reminders**.
2. Turn on **Send reminders automatically**.
3. Add up to four reminders. For each, choose when it goes, such as **3 days before the due date** or **7 days after**, and edit the message. The placeholders in braces, such as `{invoice_number}` and `{amount_due}`, are filled in for each invoice.
4. Choose whether to copy yourself on each reminder.
5. Select **Save**.

## Check that it worked

Each unpaid invoice shows the date of its next reminder under **Reminders** in the invoice's side panel. Sent reminders appear in the invoice's history with the time they went.

## Turning reminders off for one customer

Open the customer under **Contacts**, and then turn off **Payment reminders** for them. Their invoices still show as overdue in your reports; they just don't get the emails.
