# What's new in Ledgerly: November 2026

This month's release brings automatic payment reminders to every plan, a faster bank reconciliation, and two changes to sign-in.

## New

- **Payment reminders on every plan.** Reminders were part of the Plus plan. They're now included for everyone. See *Send payment reminders automatically*.
- **Suggested matches in reconciliation.** When a bank transaction and an invoice have the same amount and a date within three days, Ledgerly proposes the match. You confirm it in one step.

## Changed

- **Sign-in sessions last 14 days** instead of 30. You're asked to sign in again a little more often.
- **Two-step verification can be required by an administrator** under **Settings** > **Security**. Everyone in the company then sets it up at their next sign-in.

## Fixed

- The profit and loss report counted a credit note twice when it was applied to an invoice from a previous year.
- Attachments larger than 10 MB failed to upload on some browsers without a message. They now upload, and the limit is 25 MB.

## Coming in January

A new sign-in page, with the same addresses and passwords. See *Upcoming change: new sign-in page in January* for what looks different.
