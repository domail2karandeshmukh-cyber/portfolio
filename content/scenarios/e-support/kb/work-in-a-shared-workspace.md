# Work in a shared workspace

A workspace is one set of books that several people work on. Everyone in it sees the same data at the same time, and Ledgerly keeps you from writing over each other's work.

## Who sees what

A person's role decides what they see. A **Viewer** sees reports only; a **Bookkeeper** sees and edits transactions; an **Administrator** also manages users and settings. Roles are set per workspace, so the same person can be an administrator in one workspace and a viewer in another.

## Editing at the same time

If two people open the same invoice, the second one sees **Someone else is editing this** with the first person's name, and can read but not save. The lock lifts as soon as the first person leaves the invoice.

## Switching workspaces

Select the workspace name in the top-left corner to switch. Your recent workspaces are listed first. The search and the reports always show the workspace you're in, never all of them at once.

## Knowing who did what

**Settings** > **Activity** lists every change with the person, the time, and what changed. Select an entry to see the values before and after.
