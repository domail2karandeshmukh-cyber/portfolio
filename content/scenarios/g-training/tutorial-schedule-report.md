# Schedule a report for automatic delivery

Send a report to the people who need it, every week or every month, without opening {{product}} on the day. You choose the report, the recipients, the day and time, and the format, and {{product}} sends it as an attachment from then on. The whole task takes two minutes. Fictional sample written for this portfolio: {{product}} is an invented application.

## Before you start

- You can open the report you want to send. A schedule sends the report with your permissions, so recipients see what you see.
- You have the recipients' email addresses. They don't need a {{product}} account to receive the report.

## Steps

1. Open the report, and then select **Schedule** in the top-right corner.
2. In **Recipients**, enter each address and press Enter after it. The address appears as a chip under the field.
3. In **Repeat**, choose **Weekly** or **Monthly**, and then choose the day. For a weekly schedule, choose the weekday; for a monthly one, choose the day of the month.
4. In **Time**, choose when the report is sent. The time is shown in your time zone, and the report uses the data available at that moment.
5. In **Format**, choose **PDF** for a report people read, or **Spreadsheet** for one they work with.
6. Select **Preview** to see what the recipients get: the subject line, the first page, and the attachment's name.
7. Select **Save schedule**.

## Check that it worked

A message confirms the schedule and names the next delivery, such as **Next delivery: Monday 24 November, 08:00**. The report's **Schedules** tab lists the schedule with its recipients and its next run. Recipients get the first email at the next scheduled time.

## Change or stop a schedule

On the **Schedules** tab, select the schedule, and then select **Edit** to change it or **Pause** to stop it without deleting it. A paused schedule keeps its settings and shows **Paused** in the list. Select **Delete** to remove it for good.

## Tips

- A report sent to many people is a good candidate for **PDF**: it looks the same everywhere, and nobody can change the numbers by mistake.
- Schedules run in your name. If you leave the team, ask a colleague to create the schedule again under their own account before yours is closed.
- The **Preview** uses today's data. The real delivery uses the data of the day it's sent.
