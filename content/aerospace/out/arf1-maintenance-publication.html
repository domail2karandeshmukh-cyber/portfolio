General

The ARF-1 training rig is a bench-mounted device for maintenance training. It has an air recirculation fan, an air filter and a control box.

Students use the rig to practice removal, installation, examination, adjustment and fault isolation tasks. The rig does not represent a specific aircraft.

Main components

Refer to Figure 1 for the location of the components.

The rig has these main components:

  • The frame and the access panel

  • The fan assembly

  • The air filter

  • The airflow indicator

  • The control box

Fan assembly

The fan assembly pulls air through the air filter. Then it pushes the air through the outlet duct and the airflow indicator.

Before Modification M01Before Modification M01, four screws and four washers attach the fan assembly to the frame.

After Modification M01After Modification M01, two quick-release latches attach the fan assembly to the frame. You do not need tools to open the latches.

A connector connects the fan assembly to the control box.

Air filter

The filter element is in the filter housing. A clamp holds the cover of the filter housing.

A differential pressure indicator is on the filter housing. When the filter element is blocked, the indicator shows a red band.

Control box

The control box supplies electrical power to the fan assembly. It has these controls and indicators:

  • Power switch S1

  • Circuit breaker CB1

  • Speed control R1

  • POWER lamp DS1 (green)

  • FAN FAULT lamp DS2 (amber)

  • Test points TP1 and TP2

An adjustment potentiometer R2 inside the control box sets the maximum fan speed.

Procedure

Safety conditions

WARNING

Disconnect the bench power supply before you touch the fan assembly. The fan blades can turn and cause injury to your fingers.

Fault isolation

  1. 1

    Connect the bench power supply. Set the power switch S1 to ON. Refer to Figure 1.

    Does the POWER lamp DS1 come on?

    Yes: go to Step 3No: go to Step 2

  2. 2

    Look at the circuit breaker CB1.

    Is the button of CB1 out?

    Yes: go to Step 6No: go to Step 7

  3. 3

    Turn the speed control R1 to MAX.

    Does the fan run?

    Yes: go to Step 8No: go to Step 4

  4. 4

    Look at the FAN FAULT lamp DS2.

    Does DS2 come on?

    Yes: go to Step 5No: go to Step 9

  5. 5

    Set the power switch S1 to OFF. Disconnect the bench power supply. Disconnect the fan harness connector from the fan assembly. Examine the pins of the connector.

    Are all pins straight and clean?

    Yes: go to Step 10No: go to Step 11

  6. 6

    End of the procedure.

    Push the button of CB1 to reset it. If CB1 opens again, replace the control box. Then do this procedure again.

  7. 7

    End of the procedure.

    Make sure that the bench power supply is set to 24 V DC and is on. If it is, replace the control box.

  8. 8

    End of the procedure.

    The fan runs at MAX. Do the function test of the fan assembly. Refer to Fan assembly - Function test ARFRIG-A-20-00-00-00A-340A-A.

  9. 9

    End of the procedure.

    Replace the control box. Then do the function test of the fan assembly.

  10. 10

    End of the procedure.

    Replace the fan assembly. Refer to Fan assembly - Remove procedure ARFRIG-A-20-00-00-00A-520A-A and Fan assembly - Install procedure ARFRIG-A-20-00-00-00A-720A-A.

  11. 11

    End of the procedure.

    Clean or straighten the pins. If a pin is broken, replace the connector. Then do this procedure again.
