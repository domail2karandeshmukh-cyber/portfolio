# **Gmail FAQs**

## Overview

This document provides FAQs for Gmail. It includes separate sections for employee-facing and admin-facing questions.

## End User FAQs

**How do I compose and send an email in Gmail?**  
To compose and send an email, follow these steps:

1. Open Gmail  
2. Click the **Compose** button  
3. Enter the recipient’s email address, subject, and message  
4. Click the **Send** button to send your email

**Can I attach files to my Gmail emails?**  
 Yes. To attach files, follow these steps:

1. Open a new compose window in Gmail  
2. Click the **paperclip** icon to attach files from your computer  
3. Or select files from Google Drive to attach larger files  
4. Attach multiple files as needed

**How do I organize my emails in Gmail?**  
To organize your emails, follow these steps:

1. Use labels to categorize and sort emails  
2. Create filters to automatically sort incoming emails  
3. Use labels as folders to keep emails organized  
4. Use the search function to quickly find emails

**Can I access Gmail on my mobile device?**  
Yes. To access Gmail on your mobile device, follow these steps:

1. Download the Gmail app from the Google Play Store or Apple App Store  
2. Open the app and log in with your Gmail account  
3. Access your emails from your Android or iOS device

**How do I search for emails in Gmail?**  
To search for emails, follow these steps:

1. Enter keywords, sender name, subject, or other details in the search bar at the top of Gmail  
2. Use search operators (like **`from:`** or **`subject:`**) to refine your search

**Can I use Gmail offline?**  
Yes. To use Gmail offline, follow these steps:

1. Open Gmail  
2. Click the **Settings (gear)** icon in the top-right corner  
3. Select **See all settings**  
4. Click the **Offline** tab  
5. Check the box to enable offline access  
6. Read, write, and search your emails without an internet connection

**How do I set up email forwarding in Gmail?**  
To set up email forwarding, follow these steps:

1. Open Gmail  
2. Click the **Settings (gear)** icon and select **See all settings**  
3. Go to the **Forwarding and POP/IMAP** tab  
4. Click **Add a forwarding address**  
5. Enter the email address you want to forward emails to  
6. Follow the prompts to confirm and enable forwarding

**Is it possible to schedule emails to be sent later in Gmail?**  
Yes. To schedule emails, follow these steps:

1. Compose a new email in Gmail  
2. Click the **arrow** next to the **Send** button  
3. Select **Schedule send**  
4. Choose the date and time for the email to be sent

**How do I set up email signatures in Gmail?**  
To set up email signatures, follow these steps:

1. Open Gmail  
2. Click the **Settings (gear)** icon and select **See all settings**  
3. Go to the **General** tab  
4. Scroll to the **Signature** section  
5. Enter your desired signature text and format it  
6. Choose whether to include the signature in new emails, replies, or both
