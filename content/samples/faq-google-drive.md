# **Google Drive FAQs**

## Overview

This document provides FAQs for Google Drive. It includes separate sections for employee-facing and admin-facing questions.

## End User FAQs

**How do I upload files to Google Drive?**  
To upload files, follow these steps:

1. Open Google Drive in your web browser or open the Google Drive app on your device  
2. Click the **\+ New** button  
3. Select **File upload** or **Folder upload**  
4. Choose the files or folders you want to upload from your computer

**Can I access my files in Google Drive offline?**  
Yes. To access your files offline, follow these steps:

1. Open Google Drive in Chrome  
2. Click the **Settings (gear)** icon in the top-right corner  
3. Select **Settings**  
4. Scroll down and check the **Offline** box to enable offline access  
5. Access and edit your synced files without an internet connection

**How do I share files and folders with others in Google Drive?**  
To share files and folders, follow these steps:

1. Right-click the file or folder you want to share  
2. Select **Share**  
3. Enter the email addresses of the people you want to share with  
4. Choose their access level (view, comment, or edit)  
5. Decide whether they can share with others

**Can I collaborate on files in real time using Google Drive?**  
Yes. When you share a file with others, collaborators can view, comment, or edit the file simultaneously. Changes are synced automatically and visible to all participants in real time.

**How do I organize my files and folders in Google Drive?**  
To organize your files and folders, follow these steps:

1. Create folders for different categories  
2. Move files into their respective folders  
3. Use color labels, star icons, or search filters to organize and locate files easily

**Is it possible to recover deleted files in Google Drive?**  
Yes. To recover deleted files, follow these steps:

1. Open the **Trash** folder in Google Drive  
2. Locate the file you want to restore (files remain in Trash for 30 days)  
3. Right-click the file and select **Restore**

**Can I sync files and folders from my computer to Google Drive?**  
Yes. To sync files and folders, follow these steps:

1. Download and install the Google Drive desktop app  
2. Open the app and sign in with your Google account  
3. Select the files and folders you want to sync  
4. The app automatically syncs your selected items between your computer and Google Drive

**How do I search for files and use advanced search features in Google Drive?**  
To search for files, follow these steps:

1. Enter keywords in the search box at the top of Google Drive  
2. Use filters to refine your search by file type, owner, or date modified  
3. Use advanced search operators for specific searches (such as **`type:pdf`** or **`owner:me`**)
