# **Google Slides FAQs**

## Overview

This document provides FAQs for Google Slides. It includes separate sections for employee-facing and admin-facing questions.

## End user FAQs

**How do I create a new presentation in Google Slides?**  
To create a new presentation, follow these steps:

1. Open Google Drive.  
2. Select the **\+ New** button.  
3. Select **Google Slides**.  
4. Start working on the new blank presentation.

**Can I import PowerPoint files into Google Slides?**  
Yes. To import PowerPoint files, follow these steps:

1. Open a Google Slides presentation.  
2. Select the **File** menu.  
3. Select **Import**.  
4. Choose the **Upload** tab and upload your PowerPoint file.  
5. Google Slides converts the file to Slides format.

**How do I apply themes and change the design of my slides in Google Slides?**  
To apply or change a theme, follow these steps:

1. Open your presentation in Google Slides.  
2. Select the **Slide** menu.  
3. Select **Change theme**.  
4. Choose from the available themes.  
5. To customize, adjust the theme colors, fonts, or background using the **Customize** option.

**Is it possible to add animations and transitions to my slides in Google Slides?**  
Yes. To add animations or transitions, follow these steps:

1. Select the slide or object you want to animate.  
2. Select the **Slide** menu.  
3. Choose **Animations**.  
4. Select the animation or transition effect you want.

**How do I collaborate with others on a Google Slides presentation?**  
To collaborate on a presentation, follow these steps:

1. Open the presentation in Google Slides.  
2. Select the **Share** button in the top-right corner.  
3. Enter the email addresses of the collaborators.  
4. Choose their access level (view, comment, or edit).  
5. Collaborators can edit and see changes in real time.

**Can I present my Google Slides presentation in fullscreen mode?**  
Yes. To present in fullscreen mode, follow these steps:

1. Open the presentation in Google Slides.  
2. Select the **Present** button in the top-right corner.  
3. Use the arrow keys or presenter mode to navigate through the slides.

**How do I export or download my Google Slides presentation as a PowerPoint or PDF file?**  
To download your presentation, follow these steps:

1. Open the presentation in Google Slides.  
2. Select the **File** menu.  
3. Select **Download**.  
4. Choose the file format (PowerPoint .pptx or PDF .pdf).

**Can I add speaker notes to my Google Slides presentation?**  
Yes. To add speaker notes, follow these steps:

1. Open the presentation in Google Slides.  
2. Select the **View** menu.  
3. Select **Speaker notes**.  
4. Enter your notes in the panel that appears at the bottom.

**How do I embed YouTube videos into my Google Slides presentation?**  
To embed a YouTube video, follow these steps:

1. Open the presentation in Google Slides.  
2. Select the **Insert** menu.  
3. Select **Video**.  
4. Choose **YouTube**.  
5. Search for the video or paste its URL.  
6. Insert the video into your slide.

**Is it possible to automate slide transitions and timings in Google Slides?**  
Yes. To automate transitions and timings, follow these steps:

1. Open the presentation in Google Slides.  
2. Select the **Slide** menu.  
3. Select **Change transition**.  
4. Set the transition effect and duration.  
5. Go to the **Present** menu and choose **Set up show**.  
6. Customize slide timings and transitions for automated playback.
