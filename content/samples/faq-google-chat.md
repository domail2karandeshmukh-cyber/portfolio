# **Google Chat FAQs**

## Overview

This document provides FAQs for Google Chat. It includes separate sections for employee-facing and admin-facing questions.

## End user FAQs

**How do I start a chat in Google Chat?**  
To start a chat, follow these steps:

1. Open Google Chat.  
2. Select the **Chat** tab in the left sidebar.  
3. Select the **\+ Compose** button or use the search bar to find a person or group.  
4. Type your message and press Enter to send it.

**Can I have group conversations in Google Chat?**  
Yes. To start a group conversation, follow these steps:

1. Open a new chat in Google Chat.  
2. Add multiple participants by entering their names or email addresses.  
3. Start chatting with multiple people in one conversation.

**Is it possible to share files in Google Chat?**  
Yes. To share files, follow these steps:

1. Open a chat or room in Google Chat.  
2. Select the **attachment (paperclip)** icon in the chat window.  
3. Upload a file from your computer, share from Google Drive, or insert a file from integrated services like Dropbox or Box.

**Can I format text in Google Chat?**  
Yes. Google Chat supports basic text formatting using shortcuts:

* Use \* for **bold**  
* Use \_ for *italic*  
* Use \~ for strikethrough

**How do I search for messages in Google Chat?**  
To search for messages, follow these steps:

1. Select the **search bar** at the top of Google Chat.  
2. Enter your search terms.  
3. Review the displayed results for messages and conversations.  
4. Use advanced search operators to refine your search.

**Can I create rooms or group chats in Google Chat?**  
Yes. To create a room or group chat, follow these steps:

1. Open Google Chat.  
2. Select the **\+ Create Room** button in the left sidebar.  
3. Name the room, add participants, and select the desired settings.  
4. Use the room for team collaboration, including messages and file sharing.

**How do I set my availability status in Google Chat?**  
To set your availability, follow these steps:

1. Select your **profile picture** in the top-right corner.  
2. Select **Set status**.  
3. Choose a status option (Available, Busy, or Away).

**Is it possible to make voice or video calls in Google Chat?**  
Yes. To make a voice or video call, follow these steps:

1. Open a chat or room in Google Chat.  
2. Select the **camera** icon to start a video call.  
3. Or select the **phone** icon to start a voice call.  
4. Calls integrate with Google Meet for seamless video and audio.

**Can I use Google Chat on my mobile device?**  
Yes. To use Google Chat on mobile, follow these steps:

1. Download the Google Chat app from the Google Play Store or Apple App Store.  
2. Open the app and log in with your Google Workspace account.

3. Access your chats and collaborate from your Android or iOS device.
