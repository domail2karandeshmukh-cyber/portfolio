# **Google Meet FAQs**

## Overview

This document provides FAQs for Google Meet. It includes separate sections for employee-facing and admin-facing questions.

## End User FAQs

**What is Google Meet?**  
Google Meet is a video conferencing and online meeting platform. It allows you to host and join virtual meetings, collaborate through audio and video, and share screens and files.

**How do I start a meeting in Google Meet?**  
To start a meeting, follow these steps:

1. Go to [**meet.google.com**](https://meet.google.com) or open the Google Meet app  
2. Click the **\+ Join or start a meeting** button  
3. Choose to start a new meeting  
4. Share the meeting link with participants or invite them directly

**Can I schedule a meeting in Google Meet?**  
Yes. To schedule a meeting, follow these steps:

1. Open Google Calendar  
2. Create a new event  
3. Add a Google Meet video conference link to the event  
4. Save the event and send invites so participants can join by clicking the link

**How many participants can join a Google Meet meeting?**  
The number of participants depends on your Google Workspace plan. The free version supports up to **100** **participants**, while Business and Enterprise editions allow more participants.

**Is it possible to record a meeting in Google Meet?**  
Yes. To record a meeting, follow these steps:

1. Join or start a meeting in Google Meet  
2. Click the **three-dot menu** in the bottom-right corner  
3. Select **Record meeting**  
4. The recording is saved to Google Drive and shared with meeting participants

**How do I share my screen in Google Meet?**  
To share your screen, follow these steps:

1. Join or start a meeting in Google Meet  
2. Click the **Present now** button in the bottom-right corner  
3. Choose to share your entire screen, a specific application window, or a Chrome tab

**Can I integrate Google Meet with other Google Workspace applications?**  
Yes. Google Meet integrates with other Google Workspace apps. You can schedule or join meetings from Google Calendar, share files from Google Drive, and collaborate on Docs, Sheets, or Slides during meetings.

**Is it possible to join a Google Meet meeting without a Google account?**  
Yes. Participants can join without a Google account. The meeting organizer generates a link, and guests can join as visitors without signing in.

**How do I use captions or subtitles in Google Meet?**  
To turn on captions, follow these steps:

1. Join a meeting in Google Meet  
2. Click the **three-dot menu** in the bottom-right corner  
3. Select **Turn on captions** to view real-time subtitles of spoken words

**Can I use Google Meet on my mobile device?**  
Yes. To use Google Meet on your mobile device, follow these steps:

1. Download the Google Meet app from the Google Play Store or Apple App Store  
2. Open the app and log in with your account  
3. Join or host meetings from your Android or iOS device
