Reset your password

If you’ve forgotten your Ledgerly password, or you want to change it, you can set a new one in a minute. You need access to the email address you sign in with.

Before you begin

  • Know the email address of your Ledgerly account. The reset email goes there and nowhere else.
  • If your company signs in through its own identity provider, you have no Ledgerly password. Change it where you change your work password, or ask your IT team.

Procedure

  1. On the sign-in page, select Forgot your password?
  2. Enter your email address, and then select Send reset link.
  3. Open the email from Ledgerly and select Set a new password. The link works for 30 minutes.
  4. Enter a new password twice. It needs at least 12 characters, and it can’t be one you’ve used before.
  5. Select Save password. You’re signed in on this device, and signed out everywhere else.

Results

You see your dashboard, and the email address receives a message that says the password was changed. If you didn’t change it, select Secure my account in that message straight away.
