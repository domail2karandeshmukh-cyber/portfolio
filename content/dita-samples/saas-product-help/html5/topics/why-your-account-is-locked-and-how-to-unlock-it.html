Why your account is locked, and how to unlock it

Ledgerly locks an account after ten wrong passwords in a row. The lock protects your books from someone guessing your password, and it lifts on its own.

How long the lock lasts

The first lock lasts 15 minutes. If wrong passwords continue after that, each new lock lasts twice as long as the one before, up to 24 hours. A correct password after the lock lifts resets the count.

What you can do

  • Wait 15 minutes, and then sign in with the right password. This is the quickest way in most cases.
  • Reset your password if you aren’t sure of it. A password reset unlocks the account at once. See Reset your password.
  • Ask an administrator. An administrator of your company’s Ledgerly account can unlock you from Settings > Users. They can’t see or set your password.

If you didn’t enter those passwords

Someone else tried to sign in as you. Reset your password, and then turn on two-step verification under Your account > Security if it isn’t on already. You can also see the attempts, with their time and approximate location, under Your account > Sign-in activity.
