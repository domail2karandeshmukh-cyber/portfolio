Manage users and roles

As an administrator, you decide who can open your company’s books and what each person can do there. Every user has one role per workspace.

Before you begin

The roles

RoleCanCan’t
ViewerSee reports and dashboards.See individual transactions or change anything.
BookkeeperSee and edit transactions, invoices, bills, and contacts.Change settings, users, or bank connections.
AccountantEverything a bookkeeper can, plus year-end adjustments and locked periods.Change billing or users.
AdministratorEverything, including users, billing, bank connections, and security settings.

Procedure

  1. Select Settings > Users, and then select Invite.
  2. Enter the email address and choose the role.
  3. Select Send invitation. The invitation works for seven days.

What to do next

Change a role

Under Settings > Users, select the user, choose the new role, and select Save. The change applies the next time they load a page.

Remove a user

Under Settings > Users, select the user, and then select Remove. They’re signed out at once. Everything they entered stays in the books, with their name on it.

Keep at least two administrators

If the only administrator leaves, nobody can manage users or billing. Ledgerly warns you when a workspace has one administrator. Add a second one before it becomes urgent.
