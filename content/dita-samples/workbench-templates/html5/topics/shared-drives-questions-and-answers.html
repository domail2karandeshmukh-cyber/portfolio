Shared Drives: Questions and Answers

Short answers about who owns the files in a shared drive, and how to get one back.

Who Can Create a Shared Drive?

Anyone in your organization can create one, unless your administrator has turned this off.

What Happens to My Files If I Leave the Team?

The files stay where they are. A shared drive belongs to the team, not to the person who added the files.

How Do I Restore a Deleted File?

  1. Open the shared drive, and then select Trash.
  2. Select the file, and then select Restore.

A file stays in the trash for 30 days.
