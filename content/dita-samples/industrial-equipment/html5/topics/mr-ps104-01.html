Monthly inspection record for feed pump B

The record a technician completes during the monthly inspection of feed pump B, tag PS-104, under procedure MP-PS104-01. The maintenance console fills the first table from the work order; the technician fills the rest. A record is complete when every field has a value or the entry Not applicable, and the supervisor has signed it. Fictional sample written for this portfolio: it isn't a record for real equipment.
