Record the Readings for a Monthly Pump Inspection

Document ID MP-PS104-01-G · Version 1.0

Record the four inspection readings for a feed pump in the maintenance console, then close the work order. Fictional sample written for this portfolio: it isn't an operating instruction for real equipment.

Before You Start

  • You're signed in to the maintenance console as a technician on the shift the work order is assigned to.
  • You have the readings from the local gauges.

Warning: Stay outside the guard while the pump runs. Take every reading from a gauge or the console.

Steps

  1. Open the work order and select Record readings

    The work order lists the four checks and their expected values.

  2. Enter each reading as shown on the local gauge, then select Save readings

    Enter the discharge pressure in bar, the bearing temperature in °C, the vibration in mm/s, and whether the seal leaks.

  3. Check that all four results are inside their range, then select Close work order

    If a result is outside its range, don't close the work order. Report the reading to your supervisor.

Check Your Work

The work order shows Ready to close, and all four results are inside their expected range.
