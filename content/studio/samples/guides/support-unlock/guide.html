Unlock a customer's account

Document ID SUP-AG-004 · Version 2.0

Internal procedure for support agents of Ledgerly: a customer is locked out and can't wait for the lock to lift. Verify who you're talking to before you change anything. Fictional sample written for this portfolio.

Before you start

  • The customer has opened a ticket and is in the conversation with you.
  • You have the Support agent role in the support console.

Warning: Never unlock on the request of someone who isn't the account holder or an administrator of the company, however urgent the request sounds.

Steps

  1. Find the account by its email address

    The customer record opens with the ticket beside it.

  2. Check that the customer is verified in this conversation

    The green banner names the method and the time. Without it, send a verification code first.

  3. Review the recent sign-in attempts

    Attempts from the customer's usual location and device point to a mistyped password. Attempts from elsewhere mean you reset the password instead of unlocking.

  4. Select Unlock, or send a reset link if the customer isn't sure of the password

    Enter the ticket number as the reason. A reset link unlocks the account when it's used.

  5. Check the confirmation, and then record what you did in the ticket

    The customer receives an email that support unlocked the account.

Check your work

The Sign-in tab shows Unlocked by with your name and the ticket number, the customer can sign in, and the ticket note says what you did and why.
