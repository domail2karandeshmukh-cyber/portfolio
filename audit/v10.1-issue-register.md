# Version 10.1 issue register

What the Version 10.1 audit found on karandeshmukh.dev (production at commit `67e09a2`, Version 10), what was done about each finding, and what is still open. The audit started from the brief's recruiter journeys, then looked at the rendered pages on a desktop (1,440 px) and a phone (390 px).

Priority: **P1** stops a visitor from doing what they came for, or makes the work look less credible. **P2** costs a visitor time or attention. **P3** is polish.

## Recruiter journeys

| # | Journey | Finding | Priority | Status | Fix and evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | A recruiter has 15 seconds | The first screen gave 45 % of its height to a headline that edits itself, and the facts were in body text below it. There was no availability line, and three stacked buttons of different widths competed for the first click. | P1 | Fixed | Who, a two-line headline ("Complex technology. Clear documentation."), the facts, availability, two primary actions (**Explore my work**, **Download CV**) and four secondary links. All of it is in the first screen at 1,440 px and 390 px. |
| 2 | A recruiter wants to email or call | The site had no email address and no phone number; the contact section offered LinkedIn only. | P1 | Fixed | Email (confirmed by Karan) in the home page's contact section, every footer, the recruiter page and a new contact page with a subject ready for six kinds of message. No phone number: Karan chose email only. |
| 3 | A hiring manager has one minute | The recruiter page was a text link on phones and absent from the desktop first screen. | P2 | Fixed | **Recruiter overview** is the first secondary link in the hero. |
| 4 | A documentation manager wants a real user guide | Every software sample documents an invented product (Ledgerly, Lattice Cloud, Fieldbook, Acme Console) or an employer's product. | P1 | Open | Phase C: documentation of real, public products. |
| 5 | An aerospace recruiter wants a publication | The ARF-1 publications are there, but the downloads list labels files by their place in a group ("Its captions (WebVTT)", "Everything below in one package"), which means nothing in a flat list. | P2 | Open | Phase G: publication sets with previews. |
| 6 | Anyone on a phone | The April button covered the second call to action on the first screen. | P2 | Fixed | On phones the button waits until the hero's actions have scrolled away. |
