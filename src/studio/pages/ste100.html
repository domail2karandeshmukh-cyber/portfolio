Simplified Technical English checker

Check procedural or descriptive text against original rules modelled on the public summary of ASD-STE100 Issue 9, or against the site's house style. Each finding is marked in the text, with the rule, an explanation and a suggestion. Apply the suggestions to a corrected copy, then compare the two.

Not a compliance check. ASD-STE100 is a standard of ASD, free on request from asd-ste100.org. This checker doesn't reproduce its dictionary: it uses an original project word list, so a word it accepts may still not be approved. No tool can certify that a text complies with the standard, and this one doesn't say so. Translations of controlled English are not STE.
