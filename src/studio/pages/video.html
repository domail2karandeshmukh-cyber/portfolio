Video Studio

Edit a software walkthrough into a training video with captions, and render it in your browser.

Edit a software walkthrough into a training video: cut the footage, add titles, step numbers, arrows, highlight boxes, warnings, zoom and blur regions, a clear pointer with click ripples, captions and narration. Then render a video file in your browser.

The export plays the timeline once in real time and records it, so a 30-second video takes about 30 seconds. Chrome and Edge make MP4; browsers that can't make MP4 make WebM, and the studio says which. The file is checked by playing it back before it's saved. Text-to-speech and automatic transcription aren't included: record or add the narration, and write or import the captions. Shortcuts: Space plays and pauses, the arrow keys move one frame (one second with Shift), Ctrl+Z undo, Ctrl+Shift+Z redo, Ctrl+S save the project.

Editing a video needs a wider screen than this one. On a phone you can open the sample and play it; to edit and export, use a computer.
