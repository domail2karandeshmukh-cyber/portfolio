Interactive electronic technical publication

The maintenance and operator publications of the fictional ARF-1 training rig, read from their S1000D publication modules. Choose a product to see only what applies to it, follow cross-references, search the text, step through the fault isolation one question at a time, and print a module or the whole publication.

Fictional demonstration. A viewer built for this portfolio to show what an IETM does. It claims no IETM class and no conformance to the S1000D functionality matrix, and the publications are not approved maintenance data.
