Screenshot Studio

Mark up a screenshot the way a procedure needs it: arrows, numbered callouts, highlights, a spotlight, and redaction that can't be undone in the file. Export a PNG, JPEG or SVG, or save the project and keep editing later.

Open a sample below, or your own image. You can also paste an image with Ctrl+V or drop a file on the canvas.

Screen capture asks your browser for permission and takes one frame: sharing stops at once, and nothing is uploaded. Opening an image here drops its hidden metadata, such as camera, place and editing software, from every export. Shortcuts: V select, A arrow, R rectangle, N callout, T text, X redact, C crop, Ctrl+Z undo, Ctrl+S save the project, arrow keys to move the selected mark.
